## Video playback engines

- **mpv** and **libmpv** play formats the system player cannot (MKV, AVI, RTSP and others). mpv is licensed under the GNU GPL v2 or later, or under the LGPL v2.1 or later when built without GPL components. https://mpv.io
- **FFmpeg** (libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswscale, libswresample and related libraries) is licensed under the LGPL v2.1 or later, and under the GPL v2 or later where GPL components are enabled. https://ffmpeg.org
- **libplacebo** (LGPL v2.1), **libass** (ISC), **dav1d** (BSD 2-clause), **HarfBuzz**, **FreeType**, **FriBidi**, **zlib** and **GnuTLS / nettle / hogweed** (LGPL v2.1 or later) are used by the playback stack.
- **MPVKit** packages these libraries for Apple platforms. https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit

The source code of the LGPL and GPL components is available from the project sites above. The versions used by this app are available on request at the contact address on the Privacy Policy.
