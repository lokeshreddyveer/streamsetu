fpcalc, from Chromaprint 1.6.2 (https://acoustid.org/chromaprint)

StreamSetu includes Chromaprint 1.6.2 (unmodified source, vendored in Sources/CChromaprint with its LICENSE.md, using Apple's vDSP for the FFT) to make the fingerprint inside the app. Only if a file cannot be read that way does it run fpcalc, a separate program, to turn the first two minutes of a song you choose into a short acoustic fingerprint. The fingerprint and the
song's length (never the audio) are then sent to AcoustID to recognise the recording.

Chromaprint's own code is MIT licensed. It includes parts of FFmpeg under the GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1, so as a whole it is LGPL 2.1.
The source of Chromaprint is available at https://github.com/acoustid/chromaprint. fpcalc is not modified, is not linked into StreamSetu, and can be replaced
by any build of fpcalc that you install: StreamSetu looks for the one you choose in Settings first.
