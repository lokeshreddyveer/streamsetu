Karaoke vocal-removal model: Spleeter (two stems, vocals and accompaniment)

What it is
  The network that takes the vocals out of a song when Karaoke is on is Deezer's Spleeter two-stem model. The weights are the
  originals from the official release, converted without change to Apple's Core ML format (half precision). Nothing is
  retrained, and the model never leaves the device.
