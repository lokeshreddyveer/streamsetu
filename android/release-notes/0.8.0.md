**StreamSetu __VERSION__ for Fire TV / Android TV and for Android phones and tablets**

Two downloads, with the same build inside. Pick the one for your device:

- `StreamSetu-FireTV-__VERSION__.apk`: for a Fire TV or Android TV (remote layout).
- `StreamSetu-Android-__VERSION__.apk`: for an Android phone or tablet (touch layout).

Both are signed with the StreamSetu release key. SHA-256 of each file (they are the same file): `__SHA256__`

What is new
- **A new opening screen:** a "Who is watching?" picker with one large tile per library, what is in the one you are on (channels, movies, series, last read), and Settings, Manage and Details underneath.
- **Settings:** redesigned, with an icon and a line of description for every category, a header on each page, rounded groups, a clear highlight, and real on/off switches. The Home screen is lighter too: a slim header, a bigger featured banner, shortcut pills, then your rows.
- **Fire TV navigation:** pressing Left at the edge of any page opens the sidebar. New folder and Edit (manage folders) are at the top of the folder list, so there is no scrolling down to them. The folder highlight stays in one place while the list moves under it. Moving between folders is smoother: the grid starts at the top of the new folder and folders open after a shorter pause.
- **Player:** a sleep timer with 15 to 120 minutes (and the time left), a Jump panel (back or forward 10 seconds, 1 minute, 10 minutes, start over), subtitle and audio sync, and more speeds (0.25× to 3×).
- **TV Guide:** the guide is read much faster, gzip guides are recognised even without a ".gz" name, and channels past the first 3,000 are no longer left out.
- **Sounds:** new, clearer and louder tones for moving, choosing, going back, panels, favorites and playback start.
- **Mac, iPhone and download links:** all downloads now live on this one repository's Releases page.

Not yet tried on real equipment: Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, tuners, WebDAV, Chromecast, skip intro and reminders. Please tell me if anything misbehaves.

Install: on a Fire TV, use Downloader or ADB. On a phone, open the file and allow installs from this source. The app can also update itself from this page (Settings, About, Updates).

StreamSetu is a player only. It includes no channels, movies or series.
