# 2 November 2026 (2)

- **Fire TV and Android TV 0.23.1: the player menus work with the remote.** Audio and subtitles, Picture tuning, Sound, Subtitle look, Quality, Chapters, Up next, Folders, Programme and Technical information now open with the first entry selected, so the arrow keys and OK work at once. In Audio and subtitles, Right moves to the subtitle column and Down stays in the list; before, the keys went to the buttons behind the panel.

This release has the Android and Fire TV apps only. The Mac (2.15.1) and iPhone and iPad are unchanged and stay on 2026.11.02.

## Known limits

- Checked with a remote on one Fire TV for Audio and subtitles, Picture tuning, Sound, Subtitle look and Sync. Quality, Chapters, Up next, Folders, Programme and Technical information got the same change but were not opened on a TV.
- Projector mode is confirmed on a real projector.
- Opening Movies or Series for the first time after launch is still slower than the targets we set, and some key presses take 21 to 33 ms to show where 20 is the limit.
