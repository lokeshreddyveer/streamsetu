## In every app

- **A stronger player:**
  - **Search inside the player** with a menu for All, Movies, Series or Live TV.
  - **Go to an exact time**, such as 1:23:45.
  - **Remembered choices:** audio language, subtitles, speed and timing come back the next time you play the same title or channel. A switch in the player and in Settings turns it off.
  - **Skip Intro:** learns where a series opening ends from your first skip, then offers a button on every episode.
  - **Subtitles from OpenSubtitles**, using your own free key and account, kept on the device.
  - **Picture tuning** (brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma, deinterlace, software or hardware decoding), **volume boost** up to 150%, **subtitle size, colour, position and background**, and a **resolution picker** for streams that offer several.
  - **Stats overlay** with player, decoder, bitrate, buffer and dropped frames.
  - Picture tuning needs the MPV player; the app offers to switch.

- **Search inside the player:** a Search button in the player searches the whole catalog without leaving it. A menu narrows it to All, Movies, Series or Live TV, and All is the default. A channel, movie or episode starts at once. A series opens its page on Fire TV, Android TV and Android phones, and starts its first episode, or where you left off, on the Mac and iPhone.

## Mac 2.5.0

- **Home and Movies:** the big slideshow now scrolls up with the page instead of staying fixed.

- **More like this** was missing in 2.4.0 and 2.4.1 because the row never loaded. It now shows under every movie and series, with the reason under each poster.

## iPhone and iPad 0.6.0

- **More like this** now shows under every movie and series, and opening a suggestion works from any screen.
- **Favourites:** open any title from the list. Swipe or hold a row to remove it. The top bar has a Sort menu with recently added, A to Z, Z to A, newest year and highest rated, and Remove All Favourites with a confirmation.
- **Watch Later and collections:** the same Sort and Remove All. A collection can be emptied, and all collections can be deleted, each with a confirmation.
- **Watch History:** Clear Watch History at the top with a confirmation. Swipe a row to remove just that one.

## Fire TV, Android TV, Android phone and tablet 0.10.0

- Everything under "In every app".
- **Fire TV and Android TV:** Keep playing while I browse, in the player's More list. The picture closes, the sound stays, a small box shows what is playing, Play/Pause pauses it and Menu or Info brings the picture back. The channel list in the player has a search. Live channels show the next two programmes, and More has Remind me for the next one.
- **Next episode:** a five second countdown ("Next episode in 5") before it starts by itself; any key stops it. A switch in the player turns it off. Skip length now includes 85 seconds.
- **Android phone and tablet:** the same extras in the player's More sheet, plus Remind me for live channels.
