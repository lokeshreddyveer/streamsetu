## In every app

- **A new look: Cinematic.** Near-black backgrounds with soft panels, warm white text and one ember-orange accent for focus, buttons and highlights. Corners are rounder and headlines are larger. Every app starts on it once; after that, your own theme choice is kept, and the older themes are still in Settings.

## Fire TV and Android TV

- **A redesigned Fire TV.**
  - **Navigation:** the sidebar is a floating glass capsule, with a pill on the page you are on.
  - **Home:** a larger hero with a "Featured" label, fact chips and a segmented progress bar, and headings with an accent bar.
  - **Posters:** a clean wall of artwork; the title and year appear over the poster the remote is on.
  - **Movie page:** a title page, with a large poster, a big title and grouped buttons.
- **"In folder" now works from an open Movies or Series screen.** It used to do nothing when you were already on that screen.
- **Folder counts** show the whole folder ("600 of 2,958 series"), and the next batch loads as you scroll.
