## In every app

- **Search for a person.** Type an actor's or director's name and the titles they are in appear after the titles named like it. StreamSetu asks your playlists about one movie at a time, slowly and never while something is playing; Settings > Library shows how far it has got. Series casts come with the series list.
- **Queue.** Choose Play Next or Add to Queue on a movie or series. The queue plays before the next episode and is kept between launches.
- **Favorites in your own order.** Move a favorite to the front or the end from its menu.
- **Skip recap and Skip credits**, as well as Skip Intro. They use chapters named recap or credits when a file has them, and learn from the first time you skip the credits of a series.
- **For You.** A row of titles like the last few you watched, left out anything you already watched. It shows once there are enough matches.
- **Timeline** of the whole franchise on a film's page, with each film shown once however many copies the library holds, and **More like this** shows each film once.
- **Player engine for each kind of viewing.** Choose a different engine for Live TV, movies and series, and past programmes.

## Mac, Android phone and tablet, iPhone and iPad

- **Downloads.** Save a movie or an episode on the device and watch it without a connection. One download runs at a time, none runs while something is playing, an interrupted one carries on where it stopped, and a title that streams in pieces (.m3u8) cannot be saved. They are kept out of backups.

## Mac, Android phone and tablet, Fire TV and Android TV

- **Record a live channel while you watch it.** The MPV player writes the stream to a file over the connection you are already using, so it needs no second one. Recordings are listed in Settings, where they can be played and removed.

## Mac

- **Settings in the layout of Safari's**, with six tabs.
- The window buttons stay hidden until the pointer goes to the top-left corner.

## Fire TV, Android TV, Android phone and tablet 0.12.0

- **Match the TV's frame rate** (Settings > Playback, off by default): switches the TV to 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 Hz to suit the video.
- **Play the next episode by itself** now works, with a short countdown.
- **Continue Watching widget** for the phone's home screen.
- Playlists read their lists as the VLC player does, as the Apple apps do, which should bring titles that were missing.
