# iPhone and iPad 1.2, build 77 (TestFlight)

What changed since the App Store version 1.1 (build 74):

- **Home, Movies and Series open with a full-width banner.** The artwork runs up under the status bar, with the title, year, rating, genre and a short summary low on it, so more of the picture shows. The top of the artwork is no longer cut off.
- **Movie and series pages show more.** A tall picture with the title on it, then rating, age rating, year, length and quality in one row, the genres, a credits row (director or creator, main cast, country) and the story with a More button. Anything the provider does not send is filled in from the movie database when a TMDB key is set in Settings (Artwork and Details); a title with nothing available says so.
- **The page's buttons are one consistent set:** Play, and round buttons for favourite, Watch Later, queue, download and trailer.
- **Tapping the tab you are already on goes back to its start screen**, closing a title or folder you had opened in it.
- **While you scroll, the tab bar shrinks to one round button** showing the icon of the section you are in.
- **The AI assistant button is removed for now.**
- Carried over from TestFlight builds 75 and 76: App Lock covers every window and command, iCloud sync keeps what is newer, and saved logins are safe.
- App Lock, sync and saved-login changes from the 7 November release are included.

The App Store keeps version 1.1 until this build is submitted and approved.
