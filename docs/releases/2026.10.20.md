## In every app

- **Profiles.** Up to four people on one device. Each profile keeps its own favourites, collections, watch history and resume points. A kids profile keeps the folders you locked hidden and needs its own PIN to leave. Mac: the round button at the top right. Phone and TV: Settings, Profiles.
- **Sync on this network.** Devices on the same Wi-Fi share favourites, Watch Later, collections and resume points with no account and no server. Turn it on in Settings, then pair each device once by comparing six digits. (It pauses while a profile other than the first is in use.)
- **Tonight.** What is on now and coming up on your favourite channels, what is set to record, and the next episode of the series you watch.
- **Guide alerts.** Add a word, such as a sport or a team, and a reminder is set for programmes with it in the next day and a half.
- **Subscription end dates.** Playlists that report it show when they end, with a warning from a week before.
- **A channel that stops tries your other playlists.** The same channel in another of your playlists is the last thing tried.
- **Podcasts.** Search or paste a feed address, follow shows and play episodes; your place is kept.
- **Downloads can delete themselves after you watch them** (a switch, off by default).
- **An opening animation of about six seconds** on every app (tap to skip).
- **Better iCloud sync**, with the Mac 2.8.6 fix carried on.

## Mac

- **Scheduled recording** from the TV Guide: press Record on a programme. The app has to be open at that time; the Mac stays awake while it records.
- **Search by cast and by sentence.** The Movies and Series filter finds a title by an actor or director ("Cast: Mahesh Babu" shows why it matched), and understands "telugu movies 2024 under 2 hours I haven't watched". Settings, Library, Cast and directors has **Read Cast Now** to read cast four at a time.
- Playlists stay in the bottom left; the top right is for profiles.

## iPhone and iPad

- **A Continue Watching widget** and a **Live Activity** that shows a download's progress on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- **SharePlay**: Watch Together from the player for a film or live channel (needs the system player).
- Profiles, Tonight, Subscriptions and Alerts, and Podcasts are under More.

## Android phone and tablet, Fire TV and Android TV

- **Scheduled recording** from the TV Guide (Record), set with the system alarm and saved to the recordings folder. Android may ask to allow alarms.
- A **Tonight on your channels** row on Home.
- New Settings pages: Profiles, Podcasts, Sync on this network; and Guide alerts, Scheduled recordings and Subscriptions in their sections.
