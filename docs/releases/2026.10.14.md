## Fire TV and Android TV 0.9.1

- **Opening screen:** a "Who is watching?" picker with one large tile per library, what is in it, and Settings, Manage and Details underneath.
- **Settings:** redesigned, with an icon and description for each category and real on and off switches.
- **Navigation:** pressing Left at the edge of any page opens the sidebar. New and Edit for folders are at the top of the folder list. The folder highlight stays in one place while the list moves under it, and holding Up stops at the first folder. The folder search field appears only on Live TV.
- **Player:** Back hides the controls first; a second Back asks about leaving. A sleep timer (15 to 120 minutes), a Jump panel (10 seconds to 10 minutes), audio and subtitle sync, and speeds from 0.25× to 3×.
- **TV Guide:** read much faster, gzip guides recognised, and channels past the first 3,000 are no longer left out.
- **Sounds:** new, clearer tones.
- **Updates:** the app now reads this single release, so later versions install from Settings, About, Updates. Copies older than 0.9.1 look for the old per-app releases and need this one installed by hand once.
