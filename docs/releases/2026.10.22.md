## In every app

- **Trakt.** Sign in with your own Trakt app keys (a Client ID and Secret from trakt.tv/oauth/applications). StreamSetu tells Trakt when you start and stop a title, and one watched to 80% is marked watched. Your Trakt watchlist can be copied into Watch Later; only titles that are in your library are added. Nothing is sent while you are signed out or in a kids profile.
- **Debrid.** Paste a link and play it through Real-Debrid, AllDebrid or Premiumize, with your own token. Magnet links work on Mac, Android and Fire TV; on iPhone and iPad only hoster links are accepted, because of the App Store rules.
- **Your own AI.** Choose Apple Intelligence (Mac, iPhone and iPad, runs on the device), Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Groq, NVIDIA, Ollama Cloud, or Ollama on your own network (Android and Fire TV have all of these except Apple Intelligence). The key stays in the Keychain or the device's encrypted storage and is sent only to the service you chose. Each provider's price and free limit are shown beside its setting, as of October 2026. Only the words you type are sent.
- **The search box takes commands.** Type "go to live tv", "play Inception", "resume", "last channel", "add Jersey to favourites" or "remove Jersey from watch later" and choose the row that appears. Nothing is done until you choose it.
- **Smaller fixes from two code reviews.** A wrong PIN is slowed down after several tries. On Android and Fire TV each playlist keeps its own guide ids, so two playlists that use the same channel ids no longer mix their guides.

## Mac, iPhone and iPad

- **Setu, an assistant.** A round button at the bottom right opens a chat. Ask what to watch ("a tense thriller under 2 hours", "date night", "play something best series"), about a name ("bigg boss"), what is on TV, why a stream is buffering, how a setting works, or tell it to change something.
  - A name you type on its own is answered at once by the app's own search of live channels, movies, series, episodes and the guide, with no AI to wait for. If that was not what you meant, "Ask AI" hands the same words to your AI.
  - A description ("best horror movies", "films like Athadu", "marvel in order") goes to your AI, which names real titles in order. The app matches each one against your library and shows what you have, with "Other titles not found" listed apart. Nothing that is not in your library is shown as available.
  - It knows 60 settings and features, with what each does, where it is and its current value, and can change 45 of them (subtitles, autoplay, theme, guide hours and so on). The other 15, such as lock, proxy, VPN, sync, accounts and resets, it only explains and never changes.
  - It looks at the player's real numbers to say whether a problem is the app, the provider, the network, the login or the device, checks a playlist or its guide, and recommends a player engine.
  - Everything it suggests is a button you tap. A tick appears only when it worked: playing waits until the stream is really playing, and a failure says why. Each button is checked again when you tap it (who is using the app, folder locks, what is playing), and suggestions are cancelled if the profile changes. It is switched off in a kids profile.
  - An eye button controls whether Setu may see which screen you are on, what is playing and a few library counts. It never sees playlist addresses, logins or tokens.
  - A menu lets you change model. If one fails or is out of its free limit, Setu tries the next one you have set up. Turn off "Allow online AI services" to stay on the device.
- **A smarter search box.** It reads sort and filter words ("top rated telugu movies", "newest series", "comedy not horror", "a to z"), moods ("date night", "with the kids", "something short", "binge") and names of actors, and an empty box suggests some. With an AI set up, a request that finds nothing is passed to it.

## Android and Fire TV

- The search box takes the same commands and understands the same mood phrases. For a request of four words or more, an **Ask AI** row asks your AI and lists what it suggested that is in your library, together with a search of your library using the AI's own sentence.
- Trakt, debrid and the AI settings are under Settings, Integrations.
- The Setu chat, the instant name answers and the problem diagnosis are not in the Android and Fire TV apps yet.

## What to know

- Setu was built and tried on the Mac first. On iPhone and iPad it is in this TestFlight build but has not yet been tried on a device, so tell us what you see.
- The AI answers from what the model knows, and Wikipedia for a topic. It has no live web search, so "this week's top 10" is its best recollection, not today's ranking.
- Apple Intelligence's on-device model is weak at who was in which film. For actors, use another model or add a TMDB key.
- Trakt and debrid were tested against recorded replies, not with real accounts.

## Not in this release

- On Your Device (the videos and pictures stored on the device) is written but is not in these builds.
