# 23 November 2026

- **Streaming services inside the Mac app, a faster Mac app, and the Setu assistant on iPhone and iPad.** Mac 2.18.0, iPhone and iPad 1.4 (build 79, on the App Store after review), Android phone and tablet 0.27.0, Fire TV and Android TV 0.27.0. Windows is not part of this release; it stays at 0.2.0.
- **Title pages** reworked for movies and series, and **genre shelves** on Home and Browse with an All time / Trending switch.
- **Stars on every tile,** from one shared rating rule, so the same title shows the same stars on every app.
- **Better matching of titles to artwork and details.** A second version of the matcher.
- **More artwork and details from four online sources** behind one layer: the details service, the artwork service, the episode-data service and the schedule service. Only ids go to the artwork, episode-data and schedule services, never your history, lists or folder names, and locked or hidden folders are never sent to any of them. Each source can be turned off in Settings.
- **The details service now works out of the box** with a key built into the app; you can still add your own, and you can turn it off in Settings (Artwork and Details). This replaces the older wording that said you needed a key of your own.
- **Search fixes** on every app.
- **Privacy.** The Privacy Policy and credits are updated for the services above, Sparkle and libmpv. The App Store privacy answer is updated too: the titles and years sent to the artwork and details services are declared as Other User Content (App Functionality, not linked to you, not used for tracking). These services also see your IP address. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## Mac 2.18.0

- **Streaming services inside the app.** A Services menu, which you can pin to the top bar, opens a streaming service's own website inside StreamSetu. Each service has its own icon in a themed Services panel and its own card in Settings with a Clear Data button that removes only that service's data. If a service fails to load, a diagnostics message says why.
- **Optional page options, off by default.** For one service you can choose to skip its video ads and community-marked sponsor segments. The app asks first and explains the risks, once on first open and any time in Settings.
- **Scrolling and responsiveness.** Screens are kept between tab switches instead of being rebuilt, so your scroll position, filters and search text stay as you left them; the filter list is cached; and database and player-stop work moved off the main thread. Measured on a test bench with a large catalog (20,000 movies, 5,000 series, 3,000 channels, 72,000 guide rows), before and after:
  - screen models built over 24 tab switches: 12 to 2 (movies and series), 53 to 2 (folder rows), 4 to 1 (Live TV), 4 to 1 (TV Guide);
  - opening Movies with a fresh model: 83 ms to 9 ms; Series: 16.5 ms to 4.6 ms; Movies with a search: 100 ms to 26 ms;
  - the longest pause after a tab switch, typical: about 40 ms to about 25 to 30 ms;
  - database calls on the main thread in the paths that moved: 0 (the explicit refresh and playlist actions still read on the main thread, unchanged).
- **What did not improve.** Coming back to Movies or Series now takes about 45 to 50 ms of drawing at once, against about 18 ms before, because the tiles are drawn immediately instead of after an empty frame; the total work is slightly higher by design, and nothing flashes empty. The Home tab still has a pause of about 170 ms when you switch to it, unchanged. The slowest pauses (the 95th percentile, about 175 ms) did not change.
- **Not measured.** All numbers come from an off-screen test bench. How scrolling feels on a real display, and real window frame times, are not yet measured.
- **Top bar and search fixes** for the service buttons and navigation.
- **Setu upgrade:** better answers about the app.

## iPhone and iPad 1.4 (build 79)

- **The Setu assistant is on.** The chat button sits at the bottom right and is hidden in kids mode. It was off in the 1.3 App Store build. It sends nothing until you choose a provider and key, and Apple Intelligence runs on the device.
- **Setu upgrade:** better answers about the app.
- Title pages, genre shelves, stars on every tile, better artwork matching, the four online sources and the search fixes listed above.
- Scrolling in Home, Live TV and tiles is smoother (not yet measured on a device).

## Android phone and tablet, Fire TV and Android TV 0.27.0

- Title pages, genre shelves, stars on every tile, better artwork matching, the four online sources and the search fixes listed above. The matching, metadata and guide engines were ported from the Apple apps.
- Smoother scrolling: repository updates are coalesced and tiles recompose less (not yet measured on a device).
- **Stricter secure connections.** The metadata, subtitle, watch-history, encyclopedia and AI services use https only, matching iPhone and iPad.
- Updates install over 0.26.0 as usual (the one-time reinstall applied to 0.24.0 and earlier).
