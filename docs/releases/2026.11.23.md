# 23 November 2026

- **Streaming services inside the Mac app, a faster Mac app, and the Setu assistant on iPhone and iPad.** Mac 2.18.0, iPhone and iPad 1.4 (build 79), Android phone and tablet 0.27.0, Fire TV and Android TV 0.27.0.
- **Title pages** reworked for movies and series, and **genre shelves** on Home and Browse with an All time / Trending switch.
- **Stars on every tile,** from one shared rating rule, so the same title shows the same stars on every app.
- **Better matching of titles to artwork and details.**
- **More artwork and details from four online sources** behind one layer: the details service, the artwork service, the episode-data service and the schedule service. Only ids go to the artwork, episode-data and schedule services, never your history, lists or folder names, and locked or hidden folders are never sent to any of them. Each source can be turned off in Settings.
- **The details service works out of the box** with a key built into the app; you can still add your own, and you can turn it off in Settings (Artwork and Details).
- **Search fixes** on every app.
- **Privacy.** The Privacy Policy and credits cover the services above, Sparkle and libmpv. The titles and years sent to the artwork and details services are declared as Other User Content (App Functionality, not linked to you, not used for tracking). These services also see your IP address. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## Mac 2.18.0

- **Streaming services inside the app.** A Services menu, which you can pin to the top bar, opens a streaming service's own website inside StreamSetu. Each service has its own icon in a themed Services panel and its own card in Settings with a Clear Data button that removes only that service's data. If a service does not load, a message says why.
- **Optional page options, off by default.** For one service you can choose to skip its video ads and community-marked sponsor segments. The app asks first and explains the risks, once on first open and any time in Settings.
- **Smoother scrolling and faster screen switching.** Screens are kept when you switch tabs, so your scroll position, filters, sort and search text stay as you left them. The filter list is remembered, and database and player-stop work no longer holds up the screen.
- **Top bar and search fixes** for the service buttons and navigation.
- **Setu upgrade:** better answers about the app.

## iPhone and iPad 1.4 (build 79)

- Smoother scrolling in Home, Live TV and tiles.
