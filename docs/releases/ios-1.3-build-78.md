# iPhone and iPad 1.3, build 78

What changed since the App Store version 1.2 (build 77):

**A new look and a new opening**
- **New icon.** The silver television with a play button and a teal ribbon, with a dark version for dark mode.
- **A new opening.** The first time you open the app you see the full story of the television tuning in. After that it is a short 3-second version. Settings > Appearance has an Opening setting (Automatic or Off) and a way to replay the full story. With Reduce Motion on, it is a still picture.
- **New start and setup screens,** with motion and haptics on every tap, that follow your theme.

**Home, Movies, Series and Live TV**
- **A smarter Home.** Top This Week, Trending in Your Library, Because You Watched, Rediscover and a row in your preferred language, all picked from your own library and mixed so each day looks a little different. The Shuffle button on Home mixes them again without refreshing your library.
- **Folders.** Movies, Series and Live TV open as rows, one per folder, with a strip of titles, See all and a More tile at the end. A switch in the sort menu brings back the old grid. Live TV shows what is on now, with a progress bar.
- **Three quick taps on a tab refresh your library.**
- **Titles without a picture** get a neat tile with the title and year instead of an empty box, and pictures that fail to load try again by themselves.

**Movie and series pages**
- **Other languages and versions.** If the same film or series is in your library in another language, a row shows each version. Tap one to open that version.
- **Cast.** A row of round photos with each person's name and role. Tap a person to see their films and series in your library. Photos need a free TMDB key (Settings > Artwork and Details).
- **Don't Recommend This.** A title in Continue Watching can be kept out of your recommendations. Your watching history is not touched.

**Privacy**
- **Locked and hidden folders stay private everywhere:** counts, search, recommendations, the widget, cast and similar titles. Locking a folder takes effect straight away.
- **Nothing from a locked or hidden folder is ever sent to TMDB,** and the details saved for it are removed when you lock it.
- **TMDB.** The TMDB logo and notice are in About. Weekly trending is on by default but only works once you add your own TMDB key. Saved TMDB details are removed after 180 days.
- **The app's downloads, including subtitles from OpenSubtitles, follow your proxy setting.**

**Smaller fixes**
- The empty Favourites screen is laid out properly.
- Your library is kept as it is when the app updates. The first start prepares some new indexes in the background.

The App Store keeps version 1.2 until this build is submitted and approved.
