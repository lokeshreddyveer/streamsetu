## In every app

- **Subtitles by themselves.** Turn on Fetch automatically under Subtitles (it needs your free OpenSubtitles account). A few seconds into a film or episode with no subtitles in your language, the most downloaded one is loaded. Episodes are matched by season and number, and live TV is left alone.
- **Quality and sound badges** on film and series pages: 4K, 1080p, HDR, Dolby Vision, Atmos, 5.1 and more, read from the title.
- **First-run steps.** The first screen now says what happens after you add a playlist.

## Mac, iPhone and iPad, Android phone and tablet

- **Share link.** Share a channel, film or series from its menu. The link opens it by name on another device that has StreamSetu and the same title in its playlist.

## Fire TV, Android TV, Android phone and tablet

- **Search keeps your results.** In Movies and Series, open a title from the search results and press Back: the search is there again with the same words and results, instead of returning to the empty page.
- **Interface sounds are off by default.** Turn them on under Settings > Interface sounds. There are four new sets of tones: Wood, Crystal, Soft and Arcade, next to Signature and Minimal.
- **Your own guide (EPG) address.** Settings > Channel Manager > Guide (EPG) address: paste an XMLTV link for a connection and the guide is read at once.
