# 27 November 2026

- **A new opening, a redesigned Get Set Up, and the three Mac downloads.** Mac 2.19.0: a separate download for Apple silicon, one for Intel, and the universal one.
- **Mac opening.** On pure black, the destination icons (Live TV, Movies, Series, Podcasts, Music, Sports, Services, Library) cross the screen one at a time with their names, each lighting in your accent colour at the centre. They then come together, spin and become the StreamSetu symbol, and the name slides out from behind it. Every launch after the first is a short version: the symbol turns once, as in the top bar, and the name slides out. A still picture with Reduce Motion.
- **Get Set Up** is now a full-window, three-step screen in your theme: Music, Podcasts and your streaming services, with large tiles and the services' own icons. Open it any time from Help, Get Set Up.
- **Music** (Mac): Listen Now built only from what you play, Explore by country and language, Recently Added from your artists, a redesigned Settings, smoother crossfades and Automix, names in their own script, and many fixes across the screens.
- **Podcasts** (Mac): charts for the country you pick and a row for your categories.
- **Fixes:** the opening no longer cuts out early or lets What's New appear over it; the first-run screens appear one at a time.

## Mac 2.19.0

- **Three downloads.** `StreamSetu-2.19.0.dmg` works on every Mac and is the one the app updates itself from; `StreamSetu-2.19.0-AppleSilicon.dmg` is smaller and for M-series Macs; `StreamSetu-2.19.0-Intel.dmg` is for Intel Macs. All three are signed and notarized by Apple.
- The opening, Get Set Up, Music and Podcasts changes above.
