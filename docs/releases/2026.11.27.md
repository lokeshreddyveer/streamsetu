# 27 November 2026

- **A new Mac opening and a redesigned Get Set Up, a fix for slow starts, and an Android and Fire TV update.** Mac 2.19.1, Android phone and tablet 0.28.0, Fire TV and Android TV 0.28.0, Windows 0.2.0 (unchanged), iPhone and iPad 1.4 (on the App Store).
- **Mac opening.** On pure black, the destination icons (Live TV, Movies, Series, Podcasts, Music, Sports, Services, Library) cross the screen one at a time with their names, each lighting in your accent colour at the centre. They then come together, spin and become the StreamSetu symbol, and the name slides out from behind it. Every launch after the first is a short version: the symbol turns once, as in the top bar, and the name slides out. A still picture with Reduce Motion.
- **Faster start on the Mac.** On a large library the app used to hold its window for several seconds while it tidied what the Music shelves remember. That now happens in the background, so the opening starts at once and Home, Live TV, Movies and Series open without waiting.
- **Get Set Up** is a full-window, three-step screen in your theme: Music, Podcasts and your streaming services, with large tiles and the services' own icons. Open it any time from Help, Get Set Up.
- **Music (Mac).** Listen Now: "More <language>" follows the language of the songs you play and now includes new songs in it, not only ones you have played; a language is no longer treated as a genre. Names show in their own script more often. The floating player's scrolling title stays inside the player.

## Mac 2.19.1
