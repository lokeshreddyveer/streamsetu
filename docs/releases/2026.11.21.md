# 21 November 2026

- **A new look, a new opening and a smarter Home on every app.** Mac 2.17.0, Fire TV and Android TV 0.26.0, the Android phone and tablet app 0.26.0, Windows 0.2.0, and iPhone and iPad 1.3 (build 78) on the App Store. On Android and Fire TV this version must be installed fresh once: remove the old app first (see below).
- **New icon on every app.** The silver television with a play button and a teal ribbon, on the Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets (with a dark version, and a themed icon on Android 13 and later), the Fire TV and Android TV home screen, Windows, and inside the apps.
- **A new opening.** A short story of a television tuning in. The first time after you install it lasts about 7.5 seconds; after that it is about 3 seconds. Settings has an Opening setting (Automatic or Off). With Reduce Motion on, you see a still picture instead. It cannot be skipped.
- **New start and setup screens** on every app, with motion and, where the device has it, a tap of haptics, following your theme.
- **A smarter Home.** Top This Week, Trending in Your Library, Because You Watched, Rediscover and a row in your preferred language, all picked from your own library and mixed so each day looks a little different. The top banner falls back to the poster when a title has no wide picture. Shuffle recommendations mixes the rows again and never starts a library refresh.
- **Folders view for Movies, Series and Live TV.** One row per folder, 20 folders at a time, with a More tile at the end. A Grid switch brings back the plain grid. A locked folder shows its name and a lock, nothing else.
- **Other languages and versions.** If the same film or series is in your library in another language or version, its page shows a row with each one. Tap one to open it. (Windows: in the right-click menu.)
- **Cast.** A row of people with their role on movie and series pages; tap a person to see their films and series in your library. Photos need your own free TMDB key (Settings, Artwork and Details); without one you see round monogram letters instead.
- **Weekly trending from TMDB** is on by default, but only does anything once you add your own TMDB key. What it saves is removed after 180 days. The TMDB logo and notice are in About.
- **Don't Recommend This (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and Remove from Continue Watching (Android and Fire TV).** The title leaves your recommendations or the row; your watching history is kept (on Android it is only marked).
- **Three quick taps on a tab refresh your library (iPhone).**
- **Neat tiles for titles with no picture,** showing the title and year, and pictures that fail to load try again by themselves.
- **Privacy.** Locked and hidden folders stay private everywhere: counts, search, recommendations, widgets, Watch Next, cast and similar titles. Nothing from a locked or hidden folder is ever sent to TMDB, and the TMDB details saved for it are removed the moment you lock it. On Apple devices the app's own downloads, such as subtitles from OpenSubtitles, now follow your proxy setting.

## Android and Fire TV: remove the old app first

StreamSetu for Android and Fire TV is now signed with a new key, because the old one was lost. Android installs an update only when it is signed with the same key as the app already installed, so 0.26.0 cannot install over 0.24.0 or earlier, and the update button in the old app reports that the file is not signed with the same key.

1. Uninstall StreamSetu (Fire TV: Settings > Applications > Manage Installed Applications > StreamSetu > Uninstall).
2. Install 0.26.0 from the files below.
3. Add your playlist or login again. If this device synced with your Mac, iPhone, iPad or another Android device over Wi-Fi, pair it again and your favorites and progress come back from there.

Uninstalling removes everything the app kept on that device, including backups made with Settings > Storage > Create a backup, which are stored inside the app. From 0.26.0 on, updates install over the top again.
