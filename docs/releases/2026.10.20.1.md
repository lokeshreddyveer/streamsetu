## In every app

- **Watch Privately.** Play one movie, series episode or channel without it being remembered: no history, no resume point, no statistics, nothing to sync, and no title on the lock screen. Use the ⋯ menu on a title, a long press on a tile (hold OK on a TV), or a right click on the Mac.
- **Your week.** A card on Statistics with the time you watched, how it compares with the week before, the title you watched most, new episodes and subscriptions that are ending. An optional notification comes on Sunday evening. It is worked out on the device.
- **Your usual time.** After a few days of regular viewing, the hour you usually start and what you usually put on, with one tap to play and an optional reminder ten minutes before.
- **Privacy report.** What is kept on the device and what can leave it, with each item's current state (Mac: Settings, Privacy; iPhone: More; Android and Fire TV: Settings, Privacy and Security).
- **Viewing limits for a kids profile.** Minutes a day and hours of the night. When time runs out playback stops, and the profile's PIN adds thirty minutes.
- **Start from the beginning.** A live programme that began a while ago can be played from its start through catch-up, when the channel has it.
- **Offline Home.** With no connection, Home shows the downloads that still play.
- **Now playing on your other devices.** Over Wi-Fi sync, Home shows what a paired device is playing, with Play here.
- **More with a person.** After you watch two titles with the same actor, a row of their other titles, built from the cast already read on the device.
- **Multi-view asks first** when another stream would go over what your provider allows.

## Mac

- **Record every week.** In the guide, Record Every Week repeats a scheduled recording. The app has to be open at that time.

## iPhone and iPad

- **Watch Together** in a film's menu starts the film and a SharePlay session.
