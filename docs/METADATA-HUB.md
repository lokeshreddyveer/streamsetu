# Metadata hub: services, rules and credits

One internal layer (`Services/Hub/` on Apple) combines the details service, the artwork service, the episode-data service and the schedule service. Screens never name a service. Credits for every service live in Settings only (the Credits group); no provider name appears on any content screen.

## Rules the code enforces
- Keys: the person's own key first, then the key built into the build (scrambled by `Scripts/gen-builtin-key.py`, never in a file git can see, never in a URL, never logged), else the service is off. `Scripts/check-no-secrets.sh` reads all three key files and fails the build, the Mac and Android release checks and `Scripts/test-release-gates.sh` on any leak.
- The artwork service (its terms): the project key is always sent; the person's own personal key is sent in addition (`client-key` header) and also skips the 7-day delay on new images. Express lane only: art is requested only for a title the person opens or sees (short visibility delay), one request per title until it expires (180 days), misses remembered 30 days, at most 2 requests a second and 300 a day per install, never a background queue over the library. Credit: "Artwork from the artwork service" with a link to the artwork service, in Settings > Artwork and Details and in About. Only documented API methods; no scraping; our calls are not offered to others through any API of ours.
- The episode-data service: built-in key default ON (`HubDefaults.tvdbBuiltInDefaultOn` / Android `HubDefaults.TVDB_BUILT_IN_DEFAULT_ON`). A field for the person's own key (subscriber key + PIN) always works. Stored data is kept 7 days (their README: answers may be cached "a week or even longer"), refreshed quietly. The 30-day login token is held in memory and the Keychain, only ever in an Authorization header.
- The episode-data service credit: the notice and link its terms require, shown in Settings > Credits only. No content screen shows a footer (`HubDefaults.tvdbAttributionOnScreens` is false on every platform).
- The schedule service: keyless, default on, at most 10 calls in any 10 seconds, 429 honoured (Retry-After, else 10 s doubling to 15 min). Credit "Episode and schedule data from" the service with a link, licensed CC BY-SA" with link, in Settings and About; the schedule service text stays out of shared exports and diagnostic uploads (ShareAlike).
- Failure behaviour for every service: 401/403 on the built-in key pauses it 7 days, one shared policy for the details service, the artwork service and the episode-data service (`BuiltInKeyPause`, the 7 days are `Retention.builtInKeyPause` in `Data/Cache/CachePolicy.swift`; Android keeps its own `PAUSE_MS` in `HubProviders.kt` with the same value); the person's own key is never paused, 5 consecutive failures open a breaker for 15 minutes, 5xx remembered 6 hours, offline just waits, and everything falls back silently to the details service art and the playlist's own data.
- Privacy: only a numeric id (details-service, episode-data, imdb) or a country code goes out, plus the key in a header; no title text, no profile, no device id. Locked or hidden-folder titles are never requested. Image hosts of the artwork, episode-data and schedule services see the IP like any CDN. The App Store privacy answer is decided: Other User Content, App Functionality, not linked, no tracking (see `docs/APP-STORE-PRIVACY.md`); it is no longer "Data Not Collected". The privacy policy (`legal/privacy.md`) names the three services and the credits are in `legal/licenses.md`.

## Commercial use
The artwork service's terms allow free, non-commercial use only. Adding ads, in-app purchases or any paid tier needs the artwork service's written consent first, and the details service's and the episode-data service's terms must be re-checked at the same time. Keep the artwork service account email current (owner task).

## Android wiring
The hub is live on Android for a title the person opens (the same express lane as the Apple title page; nothing runs in bulk and the artwork service is never asked about tiles). `AppContainer.hubTitles` (`HubTitles`) is asked by `rememberTitleHub` on the movie and series pages about half a second after they open: it reads the saved crosswalk, takes the details service id from the artwork matcher (`tmdb_ids`), and for a series asks the details service once for its external ids through the app's one shared the details service call (`tmdbCall`, the same pacer and express lane as every other the details service request; the hub has no the details service limiter of its own: `HubPolicy.TMDB` only carries the 180-day retention). When the details service's details reply for an opened title is fetched (`ArtworkExpress`), its ids are filed under the library item id (`extrasSink`). Then `MetadataHub.refresh` asks the artwork service (key needed) and the episode-data service (default on) for a logo and wide picture, and the schedule service for the next episode. The page shows the logo over the title (the title text stays underneath), the wide picture, and a "Next episode in 3 days" chip for a series; no provider credit line appears on the page. A title in a locked or hidden folder (or with a twin there) is asked about nowhere, the details service's ids included (`HubSendable`). Turning the details service matching off or removing the key takes back the details service facts and the details service id in the crosswalk with the rest (`AppContainer.clearTmdbData`); turning a source off in Settings deletes what it saved. Settings > Credits lists every service credit in the wording of `legal/licenses.md`.
Words that differ from the Apple chip: the far-date form reads "Next episode on 20 Dec" (the Apple text has no "on").
