## In every app

- **Franchise timeline.** Open a film that belongs to a series and a Timeline appears above More like this: every film of the series from your own library, in the order they came out, on a numbered line that is lit up to the film you are on. Choose any film on the line to open it. Dubs and other copies of the same film count once.
- **More like this** lists each film once, instead of once per dub or quality copy.
