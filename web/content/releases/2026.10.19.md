## Fire TV and Android

- **The whole library is read.** Big Xtream lists (tens of thousands of films) are read one item at a time, so a TV with little memory no longer loses part of the list. If a list breaks off part way, the library you already have is kept.
- **Movies and Series load past 600.** A folder loads 600 titles and the next 600 as you scroll near the end. The header shows both numbers, such as "600 of 2,958 series". Live TV is no longer cut at 3,000 channels. Turning on a Filter loads the whole folder.
- **Filter.** A box to find a filter by its name, and new filters: language, quality, status (not watched, started, watched, favorites, new episodes) and rating, next to release decade and connection.
- **Search keeps your results.** Open a title from the search results and press Back: the search is there again.
- **Interface sounds are off until you turn them on**, with four new sets of tones: Wood, Crystal, Soft and Arcade.
- **Your own guide address** per connection, under Channel Manager.
- **Subtitles by themselves** (Settings, Subtitles, Fetch automatically).

## In every app

- **In folder.** A movie's or series' page shows the folder it is in. Choose it to open that folder, which helps after a search.
- **Play in another app.** From the player's More menu, hand the stream to VLC, MX Player, IINA, Infuse and others. The player pauses first, so a provider that allows one connection is not asked twice.
- **Green tick and progress bars** on movies and series: a tick for what you finished, a bar for what you started. They update as soon as you stop watching.
- **Download progress ring** on the download button and on posters, filling as the title downloads, with the percentage. Not on Fire TV.
- **Quality and sound badges** on movie and series pages (4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, Atmos, 5.1).
- **Share a link** to a channel, movie or series from its menu (not on Fire TV).
- **First-run steps** on the first screen of every app.

## Mac, iPhone and iPad

- **Filters inside the Sort menu**: language, quality, status, rating and release decade. The folders menu is as it was.
- **Opening animation** when the app starts. Tap to skip.
