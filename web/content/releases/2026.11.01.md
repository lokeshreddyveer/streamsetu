# 1 November 2026

- **Fire TV and Android TV 0.21.0.** Movies and Series open with fewer database reads, and returning to a screen you just left reuses what it showed. Focus on a poster or folder is now a single immediate change, with nothing animated underneath.
- **Sidebars and sheets follow the theme.** The Settings sidebar matches the main one, with quieter icons; the folder panel puts New and Edit beside the folder name; season pills on a Series page show clearly which one is focused.
- **Artwork** is looked up again on detail pages and sheets, and for Live TV channels, and is requested a few at a time so it no longer competes with scrolling.
- **Settings, About and Diagnostics** scroll with the remote; Statistics builds its rows as you reach them.
- **Library clean-up, once.** The access token that older versions saved inside some Plex picture addresses is removed, and divider rows such as "#### SPORTS ####" are no longer kept as channels.
- The Mac app (2.15.0) and the iPhone and iPad apps are unchanged in this release.

## Known limits

- Opening Movies or Series for the first time after launch is still slower than the targets we set (first cards 335 to 551 ms on a Fire TV in our measurements). The remaining work, one combined screen state and replacing a catalog in the background instead of in one large write, is not in this release.
- A very large playlist refresh can still delay a favourite or a resume point by about a second while it is being written.
- The changes were measured on one Fire TV; Android TV and phones were unit-tested only.
