# 25 October 2026

- Fire TV and Android TV 0.19.0: a new look and a remote that behaves, with the Bigg Boss seasons and other shows that were missing now showing up.

This release has the Android and Fire TV apps only. The Mac (2.14.8) and iPhone and iPad (TestFlight build 72) are unchanged and stay on 2026.10.22.

| Device | File | Version |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire TV and Android TV** | `StreamSetu-FireTV-0.19.0.apk` | 0.19.0 |
| **Android phone and tablet** | `StreamSetu-Android-0.19.0.apk` | 0.19.0 |

## Fire TV and Android TV

- **A new look.** A charcoal theme with a white focus ring and a restrained cyan, one cinematic banner at the top of Home, Movies and Series, and rows under it (Continue Watching, Favorites, Watch Later, Recently Added, New Episodes) like the other streaming apps.
- **The sidebar hides itself.** It slides in when you press Left at the edge of a page or press Back, and slides away when the cursor is back on the page. Back on a page opens it, Back on the sidebar goes Home, and Back on Home returns to the library chooser. Back never closes the app from inside a library.
- **The cursor comes back where it was.** After a movie or series page, a menu or the sidebar, the cursor returns to the poster you left, and the pages remember their place.
- **Smoother, and no more freezes.** A held direction key is thinned to what the screen can draw, so the cursor stops when you let go, and the Guide no longer freezes the app when a channel has no guide data (each such row is now a place the cursor can stop; pressing OK plays the channel).
- **Missing shows.** Series whose episodes the provider lists in a different layout, such as Bigg Boss (Telugu) seasons 8, 9 and 10, are read and shown.
- **Folders are smaller.** In Movies and Series the category tiles are one line, "Telugu movies (2026) | 120", and the Guide's channel column is wider so names are no longer cut.
- **Live TV, Search and Settings** were redone to match: Live TV has folders, channels and a preview in three columns, the Guide opens from the player's More menu, and the Menu key opens the same menu as holding OK.
- **Library switcher.** Switch library from Home, Movies or Series without leaving the page.

## Safer behind the scenes (all Android apps)

- Changing profile now stops what was playing first, so it no longer keeps saving progress into the next profile.
- Wi-Fi sync is closed when it is switched off or the profile changes, so nothing in flight touches the next profile's data.
- Scheduled recordings: Cancel now stops the download, HLS channels are refused instead of saving a text playlist as video, and a stream that ends early is reported as partly saved, not as done.
- The guide import cuts any over-long text in a feed.

## Known limits

- Android 5.1 devices (older Fire TV sticks) cannot save a login: the secure storage needs Android 6. This is not fixed in this release.
- The Setu chat is not in the Android and Fire TV apps.
