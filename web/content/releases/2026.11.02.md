# 2 November 2026

- **Fire TV and Android TV 0.23.0, and the Android phone app.** Smart collections, better subtitle matching, Settings search, Projector mode, add-ons and a clean-up from a full review.
- **Smart collections (Fire TV and Android TV).** Make a list that fills itself: choose a language, years, a minimum rating, how recently it was added, unwatched, favourites, a connection, and words in the title or folder. All choices must hold. More, then New smart collection. Hold OK on one in the folder column to edit or delete it.
- **Subtitles are ranked (Fire TV, Android TV and phone).** Results from OpenSubtitles are ordered by how likely each is to belong to what is playing, and each says why: exact file match, same episode and running time, title match or possible match, with a confidence. A subtitle for another episode or title is left out. Automatic subtitles now load a result only when it is confident enough and is not a machine translation; before, the most downloaded one was loaded without checks. Downloads are size-limited, converted to UTF-8 and checked before use.
- **Settings search and Essential mode (Fire TV and Android TV).** Search every setting from the top of Settings and land on the exact row. New installs start on Essential, which leaves out the technical pages; existing installs stay on Advanced. Nothing is turned off by switching.
- **Projector mode (Fire TV and Android TV).** Settings, Appearance, under Theme. Off by default. Makes pages, cards, text, menus and the focus frame easier to see on a projector, and lifts the status colours. It never changes video brightness or HDR, and it is kept on this device only (not in backups or sync).
- **Add-ons (Fire TV and Android TV, Advanced).** An add-on is a small description of a service, never a program. It can only reach the servers it names, over https, to public addresses only, and its catalog (movies, series and channels) becomes a connection. Official add-ons come from a signed list; there are none yet.
- **Search, Movies and Series.** Search on the TV is one vertical list; movies, series and channels are looked up side by side. A hero banner now shows on every folder. The duplicate Search chip is gone.
- **Playlist updates** are written in small steps, so a favourite or a resume point saved during a refresh no longer waits for the whole playlist. A refresh that is cut short is redone, and two refreshes of one playlist can no longer overlap.
- **Fixes from the review.** Loading more titles in Movies and Series, rebuilding the search index, deleting a smart collection, add-on addresses and sign-ins, and restoring a Settings search all had faults that are fixed. A diagnostics page, Playback capabilities, shows what the device reports about decoders, HDR and audio (read only).
- **Mac 2.15.1.** Search can publish its results in stages (the iPhone and iPad Search screens use this in their next build); nothing you see on the Mac changes.
- **iPhone and iPad:** the App Store version is unchanged by this release.

## Known limits

- Projector mode and the new screens were tested by automated tests and on one Fire TV, not yet on a real projector. Judge the readability yourself and turn it off in Settings if it does not help.
- Opening Movies or Series for the first time after launch is still slower than the targets we set, and some key presses take 21 to 33 ms to show where 20 is the limit.
- Smart collection speed on a library of tens of thousands of titles has not been measured on a TV.
- Subtitles: OpenSubtitles does not send a running time, so "Same episode and runtime" does not occur with it. Automatic timing correction is not included. Only common UTF and Western subtitle encodings are read.
- An add-on's guide is not read in this version. There are no official add-ons yet.
- Android 5.1 devices cannot save a login (unchanged).
