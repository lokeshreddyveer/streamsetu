# 7 November 2026

- **StreamSetu for Windows 0.1.0 is here, Mac 2.16.0, Fire TV and Android TV 0.24.0, the Android phone app, and a new iPhone and iPad build on TestFlight.** Windows is a native app that plays with the same mpv engine as the Mac; the other apps get fixes to sync, App Lock, saved logins, playback and the way titles are shown.
- **Windows 0.1.0.** Connections for Xtream Codes, M3U links and files, Stalker portals, HDHomeRun, TVHeadend, Enigma2, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, WebDAV and folders of video files. Live TV, Movies and Series, the TV Guide with now and next, catch-up, Tonight and alerts, favorites, Continue Watching, collections, a play queue, filters, profiles with PINs and a Kids profile, folders you can hide, rename, reorder and lock, recording and reminders, Multi-View, a mini player, Skip intro, subtitles from OpenSubtitles, encrypted DNS, backup and restore, and sync on your network with your Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android devices. Unzip it and run `StreamSetu.exe`.
- **App Lock covers everything (Mac).** Every window, every menu command, every link and file you open, the keyboard shortcuts, the media keys and the Now Playing controls wait until you unlock; a link opened while locked runs after you unlock. On iPhone and iPad the lock cover is up from the moment the app starts.
- **iCloud sync keeps what is newer (Mac, iPhone, iPad).** Turning sync on no longer stamps old favorites with the current time, so a second device can no longer overwrite newer changes or bring back things you deleted. One item that cannot be applied no longer costs the rest of its batch; it is kept and tried again.
- **Saved logins are safe (Mac).** If the vault of saved logins cannot be opened, StreamSetu no longer starts an empty one over it. Settings shows a Saved logins row; if the vault is locked, one button moves the old files aside and starts a new set. The key for Wi-Fi sync now stays on each device and is not shared through iCloud; your Mac keeps the key it had, and an iPhone or iPad pairs again once.
- **Playback (Mac).** Changing channels quickly always ends on the last channel you chose. A memory leak of about 8 MB per channel is fixed, and the signed app loads only the player library inside it.
- **Titles and menus (Fire TV, Android TV and Android phone).** Poster and banner titles wrap to two lines and are never cut off at the bottom of a row, and every player menu opens with an entry ready to press.
- **Smart collections and New dates (Android).** "Added in N days" now reads dates correctly for Plex, Jellyfin and add-ons, so they no longer match everything.
- **Connections (Android).** Removing a connection while it refreshes leaves nothing behind, a refresh that is cancelled no longer stays on Syncing, and testing a new connection keeps one id for the whole setup. Large libraries are read in groups, which fixes Android 5 to 7. The guide address now handles passwords with special characters.
- **Kids profile and folders (Android).** A Kids profile can no longer reach Settings or Libraries, and resetting a locked folder asks for its PIN.
- **Updates and sync (Android).** The updater refuses file names that lead out of its folder and does not count a failed check. The Wi-Fi sync listener limits how many devices can connect at once.
- **Stability.** Unusual data from a provider or another device (a season listed twice, a number too large, a cut-off DNS answer, two settings for one channel, a stream address with udp or rtmp) no longer causes a crash on the Mac or on Android.
- **iPhone and iPad:** a new build (version 1.2, build 76) is on TestFlight with the sync, lock and saved-login changes above. The App Store keeps version 1.1; this release does not change it.

## Known limits

- Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" notice the first time, because the app is not signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
- Windows recording saves direct streams (`.ts`) and needs StreamSetu to be open at the time. Channels that are HLS playlists (`.m3u8`) cannot be recorded.
- Encrypted DNS on Windows also covers the player for `http://` addresses; `https://` addresses and addresses a server redirects to use the system's DNS.
- After the new iPhone or iPad build is installed, that device pairs again once for Wi-Fi sync, because its key now stays on the device.
